🧿 Boundary Traversal — Anti-Clockwise Visualizer

One guided flow, three parts. The boundary is collected in four phases: root → left boundary top-down (prefer left, fall back right, leaves skipped) → all leaves left-to-right → right boundary collected into a temp vector and reversed so it reads bottom-up. Part 1 is the tree from main(), Part 2 makes both walks fall back to their non-preferred side, Part 3 is the single-leaf-root edge case. Just press ▶ Play.

Tree
root → left flank ↓ → leaves left-to-right → right flank ↑  ·  leaves are added only by the leaves phase
boundary answer
🔵 left boundary · 🟢 leaves · 🟣 right boundary. The small number on each node is its position in the final answer. Watch temporary fill up on the right flank, then 🔄 reverse before pouring into the answer.

The boundary walks are iterative (a current pointer), while addLeaves is a recursive left-first DFS — that's what guarantees leaves come out left-to-right.

🎯 Nodes at Distance K — Parent-Map BFS Visualizer

One guided flow, two parts. Phase 1 builds a parentMap with a BFS so we can move upward — turning the tree into an undirected graph. Phase 2 runs a level-order BFS from the target in three directions (left, right, parent) and stops at distance k. Part 1 is the exact main() case (target 5, k=2), Part 2 targets the root with k=3.

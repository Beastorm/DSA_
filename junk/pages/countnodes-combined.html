🔢 Count Nodes in a Complete Binary Tree — Brute Force vs Optimal

One guided flow, four parts. The brute force visits every node (O(N)). The optimal approach compares the leftmost and rightmost heights at each node: if they're equal, the subtree is perfect and gets counted instantly by (1 << h) − 1 — without visiting its nodes (O(log²N)). Parts 1–2 use the 6-node tree from main(), parts 3–4 the 9-node example tree. Watch whole subtrees turn gold and get skipped. Just press ▶ Play.

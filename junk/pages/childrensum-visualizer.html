⚖️ Children Sum Property — Tree Mutation Visualizer

One guided flow, two parts. changeTree rewrites node values live so every non-leaf equals the sum of its children. Going down: if the children's sum is big enough, the node is pulled up to it — otherwise the node's value is pushed down into a child. Coming back up: each node is re-set to the sum of its (now updated) children. Part 1 is the tree from main() (only pull-ups happen), Part 2 uses a tree where values really do get pushed down. Watch the numbers change inside the nodes. Just press ▶ Play.

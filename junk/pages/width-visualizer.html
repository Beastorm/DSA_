📏 Maximum Width of Binary Tree — BFS Indexing Visualizer

One guided flow, two parts. Width = positions on a level including empty gaps. Give every node a heap-style index (left = 2i+1, right = 2i+2), BFS level by level, and measure last − first + 1. Subtracting each level's minIndex keeps the indices small (the overflow guard). Part 1 is the tree from main() (width 4 — one hidden gap), Part 2 shows how a deep gap stretches a level to width 8 with only 2 nodes. Just press ▶ Play.

🔥 Minimum Time to Burn a Binary Tree — Visualizer

One guided flow, two parts — exactly the two tests in main(). Phase 1: a BFS maps every child → parent and locates the start node. Phase 2: fire spreads from the start in three directions (left, right, UP via the parent map), one round per second — and the clock (maxi) only ticks when a round actually burns something new (fl flag). Part 1 starts the fire at node 1, Part 2 at leaf 4. Just press ▶ Play.
